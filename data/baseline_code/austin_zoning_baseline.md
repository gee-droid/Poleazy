# Austin Municipal Zoning Baseline

## Section 25-2-191 — Zoning Map

Section 25-2-191 establishes the zoning map for the City of Austin.

The zoning map identifies the base zoning districts and combining districts
applicable to properties within the City.

Zoning districts may include residential, commercial, mixed-use and other
district classifications established by the City Code.

## Neighborhood Commercial — LR

The LR zoning district is the Neighborhood Commercial base district.

Property developed under the LR district is subject to the applicable
development regulations and permitted-use requirements established by the
Austin Land Development Code.

## Multifamily Residence Low Density — MF-2

The MF-2 district is a multifamily residential base zoning district.

Properties within MF-2 are subject to the development regulations and
permitted-use requirements applicable to the MF-2 district.

## Combining Districts

Combining districts may modify or add requirements to a property's base
zoning district.

A conditional overlay may prohibit, restrict, or condition particular uses
or development characteristics on a property.

## Baseline Retrieval Test

Citation: § 25-2-191

The zoning map establishes the zoning classification applicable to a property.
Changes to the zoning map can change the base zoning district applicable to
that property.
